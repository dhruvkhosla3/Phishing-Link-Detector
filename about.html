Phishing Link Detector

A real-time URL threat scanner powered by pattern-based analysis.

◎

Purpose

Instantly flag dangerous URLs containing known scam, gambling, fraud, phishing, and malware-related threat indicators — before you click.

⚙

How It Works

The backend scans every part of a URL (domain, path, query) against 200+ compiled threat patterns. A single match returns an UNSAFE verdict with the exact indicators found.

⯀

Threat Categories

Gambling & rummy platforms · Financial fraud · Prize & giveaway scams · Crypto & NFT schemes · Account phishing · Malware & piracy · Adult & illegal content.

▤

Tech Stack

Backend: Python 3 · stdlib http.server · regex engine.
Frontend: Vanilla HTML / CSS / JavaScript.
No external dependencies.

✔

URL Validation

Only URLs starting with http://, https://, or www. are accepted. Anything else is rejected before scanning begins.

☰

Scan History

Every check is logged locally in your browser. Filter by safe / unsafe / error, search by URL, and clear history at any time — no data leaves your device.
